# Supported elementary indefinite integrals, PRD section 22.1 row 3.
#
# The required result is a complete derivation plus the derivative check, and the engine already
# runs that check as VER-005: it differentiates its own answer by rule and withholds an answer that
# does not come back. So what a case adds here is the answer itself, worked out from the
# mathematics, and the domain restriction where the rule carries one.
#
# The result named is the particular antiderivative, without the constant of integration, since the
# constant is a step of its own and spelling it in every case would say nothing.
